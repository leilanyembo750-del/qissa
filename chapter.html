Chapter 01

The Unexpected Meeting

A Love Written by Qadr • By Aaliyah

Maryam had always believed that some meetings were simply coincidences.

She had spent the entire morning rushing across campus, trying not to be late for her first lecture. Her books were pressed against her chest while she searched through her bag for her phone.

She wasn't looking where she was going.

And then—

She walked straight into someone.

Her books fell onto the ground.

"I'm so sorry!"

The young man immediately stepped back and began helping her collect the books.

"It's okay," he said.

Maryam quickly picked up the last book and stood.

"Thank you."

She looked at him for a moment before lowering her gaze.

"You're welcome."

There was something strangely familiar about the moment, although she couldn't explain why.

She thanked him again and continued walking.

She didn't know it yet, but that ordinary morning would become one of the most important moments of her life.

Later that afternoon, Maryam sat quietly in the university library.

She opened her notebook and tried to concentrate. But the events of the morning kept returning to her thoughts.

She smiled at herself.

"Why am I even thinking about this?"

She shook her head and returned to her notes.

Some things in life begin quietly.

You don't recognize them as beginnings until much later.
